Your coach learns from real practice

Typewell uses an on-device statistical learning model. It measures key errors, letter-pair errors, longer string errors, and pauses between keystrokes, then ranks patterns by error rate, hesitation, and sample size. It generates targeted word mixes from those patterns.

It's an adaptive pattern engine, not a cloud language model. Insights become more reliable as you practice. Long pauses above 2.5 seconds are excluded from hesitation analysis.

A few useful details

WPM is correct characters ÷ 5 ÷ minutes. Raw WPM counts every typed character, right or wrong. Accuracy includes mistakes you later fix. Results and learning stay in this browser; clearing browser data removes them. Use Settings to export your data.

tab + enter restarts a test. esc releases the typing field. On mobile, tap the test to open your keyboard.
